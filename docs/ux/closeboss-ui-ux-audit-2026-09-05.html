UI/UX audit · CloseBoss web + mobile

CloseBoss UX Audit

An objective usability, visual and AI-experience review of the CloseBoss agent product: the Next.js web app at closebossai.com and the Expo mobile app. Findings are grounded in the live production dashboard, automated WCAG scans, and the source in apps/leadsmartai and apps/leadsmart-mobile.

Date 5 Sep 2026 Account test agent 26 (Signature plan, seeded data) Viewports 1568 · 768 · 390 px live; 320–1920 via code Mobile app reviewed from source (Expo 55, v1.6.0); not run on device

01Executive summary

Overall assessment

CloseBoss has a genuinely differentiated product idea, an "AI team" you command rather than a CRM you feed, and the web app already carries the visual foundations of a modern SaaS product: a coherent OKLCH brand ramp, Geist typography, a tinted elevation scale, and a purposeful sidebar. The gap is between that foundation and what a new agent actually meets on screen. Three onboarding systems overlap, the AI's own voice leaks raw markdown and internal counters, the primary "Leads" screen is a nine-column table with icon-only actions that does not survive a phone, and the same product answers to three names (Ask Max, AI Guide, Bossy). The result sits at Functional, approaching Professional. It is not yet the calm, premium "company floor" the brand charter describes.

Current strengths

  • Strong product thesis carried into the UI. The sidebar is organised as "your day" then "your AI team", and Ask Max leads with proposals the agent approves. That is the right shape.
  • Real design tokens exist. globals.css defines a perceptually uniform brand and neutral ramp, four elevation levels, tabular numerals, and an AA-safe orange for text. The mobile theme.ts mirrors the same ramps with a hand-tuned dark palette.
  • Mobile engineering is mature where it exists. Offline banner, cached fetches, a write queue, row skeletons, haptics, accessibility labels on list rows, and a keyboard-safe login.
  • Bilingual by default. EN / 中文 across web and mobile, including per-contact language pills.
  • Approval is the control point. Autopilot is a per-assistant, per-channel matrix with "ask" as default; drafts are shown before they send.

Biggest weaknesses

  • Onboarding is three products stacked. The Max welcome interview, the Setup Wizard modal and the Site Tour all fire on a first session and ask overlapping questions.
  • AI output is not rendered as a product surface. Literal **Needs you:** asterisks, "0/25 tools" meters and a five-star "Mission accomplished" card for a clarifying question.
  • Contacts and Inbox are desktop tables squeezed onto phones. No card layout, no single-pane conversation on mobile, 16px icon-only actions.
  • Identity and naming drift. "Leads" opens a page titled "Contacts"; "Conversations" is "Inbox" in the command palette; the sidebar shows "Fan Yes" while the top bar shows "Michael Yestest"; the mobile Boss is "Bossy" with the app icon as a face.
  • Monetisation UI ignores plan state. A paying Signature user sees an orange "Upgrade" button, a "Grow with AI follow-ups, upgrade" promo, and a plan named "Subscribed".
  • No component discipline. Three primary-button colours, five spinner variants, seven radius sizes, gray and slate palettes side by side, 38 native confirm() dialogs.

Biggest risks

  • Trial abandonment during the first ten minutes: the welcome, wizard, tour and a 434-unread bell all compete for attention before the agent has seen a single lead of their own.
  • Trust erosion from AI framing: celebratory cards for non-work, and "each session starts fresh" contradicting the "chief of staff" promise.
  • Accessibility exposure: unlabeled form controls and selects (axe: critical), a drawer that cannot be closed with Escape, and a skip-link that points nowhere.
  • Mobile app cold start lands on a hidden legacy tab, so the tab bar shows no active tab.

Biggest opportunities

  • Collapse onboarding into one Max-led conversation that ends with the agent's own data on screen.
  • Make Ask Max the single AI surface (retire the floating "AI Guide" bubble) and render its output like a product, not a log.
  • Rebuild Contacts as a list with a row-level primary action and a responsive card mode; rebuild Inbox as a true two-pane that collapses on phones.
  • Ship the design system you already have: one Button, one Card, one Dialog, one Toggle, one radius scale, one neutral ramp.

Overall UX score: 5.6 / 10. To reach 8 / 10 (Professional): fix the three P0s, consolidate onboarding, make Contacts and Inbox responsive, and enforce the component library. To reach 9 / 10 (Excellent): redesign Ask Max's run cards, plan-aware monetisation, a real notification centre, and AA accessibility across every form. To reach 10 / 10 (Best-in-class): one shared design system across web and mobile, memory-backed Max, motion and micro-interaction polish, and personalised dashboards.

03Product strengths to preserve

  • The "Your AI Team" sidebar band with persona avatars: keep it. It is the clearest expression of the brand charter in the product.
  • Proposal cards in Ask Max (Proposal → reason → "Review transaction" / "Not now"): the approval loop is right. Fix the rendering, not the model.
  • Pricing page copy ("Every plan includes everything; only credits change"): unusually honest and easy to scan. Keep the structure.
  • Contact drawer "Story so far": a timeline in plain language is the right way to show AI activity per person.
  • Mobile offline and skeleton plumbing, haptics, and row accessibility labels.
  • Bilingual toggle in the top bar and per-contact language pills.
  • Save-in-button feedback (116 inline "Saved" states vs 2 toasts) matches the house rule; keep it, standardise the style.

05User journey analysis

Estimated for a new solo agent on desktop unless stated. "Clicks" counts taps, key presses to submit, and choices; typing is counted once per field.

First visit → paying trialLanding → Pricing → Agent signup → Welcome → Dashboard

Landing is ~15 sections long; hero states the thesis three times. Two CTAs ("Talk to Max", "Watch Demo") plus "Ask Max free" and "Hire Your AI Team Free" further down. Signup form: 7 fields + 2 checkboxes. Then /welcome (5 questions + 6-item plan), then the Setup Wizard modal (4 steps), then the tour prompt. ≈ 28 interactions before own data.

Add a lead3 clicks + form (desktop) · 4 (mobile)

Quick actions → Add lead → form. On mobile the Quick actions button is hidden (hidden md:flex), so the path is drawer → Leads → Add. The "Add…" button label truncates on the Contacts page.

Reply to a text3 clicks

Conversations → thread → reply. Fine on desktop. On a phone the list is squeezed to a third of the width and the reply pane may render off-screen.

Turn on the AI Receptionist2 routes, 4–6 clicks

Either Receptionist → Overview → "Voice settings" → panel, or Settings → Voice & Style → scroll past AI style and phone voice. Two doors, no signpost between them.

Approve an AI draft2–3 clicks

Ask Max → Proposal → Review → Approve. Good. But the run card that follows says "Mission accomplished" even when nothing ran.

Change notification settingsUnclear

Notifications page has no settings link; push preferences live in the mobile app and in the Setup Wizard step 4; transaction notifications live in Settings → Channels. Three homes.

Where users hesitate or abandon

  • Confusion: "Leads" in the nav opens a page titled "Contacts" with "Leads / Sphere / All contacts" chips each tagged "DEFAULT". A new user cannot tell which is the default.
  • Hesitation: Ask Max opens with four metric tiles reading "—" and a single line of greeting; the command bar sits below an empty "Business performance" disclosure. Nothing invites the first command except three small chips.
  • Abandonment: the welcome chat asks for name and brokerage that signup already collected; the wizard then asks for brand name again.
  • Mistakes: row actions in Contacts include "Mark contacted", "Draft offer" and "Start deal" as 16px icons with no confirm; one mis-tap creates a deal.
  • Recall burden: icon-only actions rely on hover tooltips, which do not exist on touch.

06Web UI audit

Shell and navigation chrome

  • Sidebar footer overlaps the nav list. At a 710px-tall viewport the pinned promo card and user card cover "Manage AI Team"; the list gains an inner scrollbar with 8px arrow buttons. P1 Fix: make the promo dismissible, move it out of the sidebar for paid plans, and give the nav list min-h-0 flex-1 overflow-y-auto with padding-bottom equal to the footer height.
  • Top bar carries seven controls (search, Upgrade, credits, EN/中文, support, Quick actions, bell, account). On desktop it works; on phones five of them vanish rather than collapse into an overflow.
  • Search placeholder promises more than it does. "Search leads, clients, addresses…" submits to the contacts list; the ⌘K palette only navigates pages.
  • Floating Max avatar (64px, fixed bottom-6 right-6) overlaps the last table row's actions on Contacts and the command bar on phones.

Page headers

Four header styles coexist: "CLOSEBOSS" eyebrow + title (Manage AI Team), plain title + subtitle (Contacts, Tasks), title + right-aligned black button (Transactions, Calendar, Listings), and tabs above title (Settings). Standardise on one PageHeader with title, one-line purpose, and a primary action slot.

Tables

Contacts, Tasks, Listings, Calls all use a hand-built <table> with its own header styling. Contacts sorts by clicking headers with a 10px triangle glyph; Tasks has no sort; Listings has none. There is no shared DataTable, no sticky header, no column visibility, no bulk action bar beyond the Contacts checkbox column.

Cards, modals, drawers

A Radix Dialog primitive exists (components/ui/dialog.tsx) but the Lead Profile drawer, Setup Wizard and most modals are hand-rolled fixed overlays. Escape does not close the drawer (verified live). 38 native confirm() calls, several with untranslated English strings ("Delete this offer? This cannot be undone.").

Empty, error and loading states

  • Empty: Transactions defaults to "Active (0)" and shows "No transactions match this filter" while "All (2)" exists. Inbox shows "Select a conversation to view" in a pane that should not exist on phones. Notifications shows two empty columns with copy that reads as an apology.
  • Loading: no loading.tsx in any of 62 dashboard route folders; 15 ad-hoc spinners in five styles; Skeleton used in five files.
  • Error: one error.tsx and an ErrorBoundary in the layout. Good baseline; the list-vs-error distinction in Inbox (listError) is a good pattern to copy.

07Mobile app audit (Expo)

Reviewed from source; the app was not launched on a simulator in this session. Findings are structural and verifiable in code.

Entry and navigation

  • Three different homes. Cold start → /(tabs)/home (hidden legacy screen; no tab highlighted). Login → /(tabs)/inbox. Onboarding finish → Inbox. Tab bar's first tab is Boss. P0 Pick Boss and route every entry there.
  • Five visible tabs, twelve tab routes. home/work/engage/analyze/manage/calendar/settings are hidden with href: null but still reachable; deep links can land on chrome-less legacy screens.
  • Calendar, Tasks and Settings are buried under More as tiles, while Team (a static roster of five rows) owns a tab. For an agent, Calendar and Tasks are daily; Team is occasional.

Boss screen

  • Entirely hardcoded English (no useTranslation) while the app ships zh-Hans; 11 other screens share the gap (briefings, cma, coaching, connect-platforms, digital-twin, expenses, tasks, weekly-schedule, offer-desk…). P1
  • Fallback name "Bossy" and avatar assets/icon.png; web is "Max" with a portrait. P1
  • One ScrollView stacks agenda, alerts, briefing, proposals and the thread above the docked command bar; the proposals, the most important content, sit below two dashboard blocks.
  • Touch targets: gear 34×34, autopilot pill ~28px tall, quick-command chips ~30px. Only 7 hitSlop uses app-wide. P1

Login and onboarding

  • Login prints the API endpoint and offers a "paste a JWT" fallback to every user. P1 Gate both behind a dev flag.
  • Value carousel has two text-only slides; the notification permission is requested before the agent has seen a lead. Defer to the first hot-lead moment.

Lists and detail

  • Leads and Inbox rows: good density, hot stripe, skeletons, a11y labels. Tapping an Inbox thread opens the lead record, not a conversation; label the row accordingly or open the conversation section anchored.
  • EmptyState has no action; a demo lead is injected instead. Add a primary CTA prop.
  • Six native Switch uses vs the product rule of a green/gray toggle next to its label; the Boss autopilot control is a custom pill acting as a switch.

Visual system

  • Feature tiles hardcode per-tile hex (#4f46e5, #16a34a, #e11d48, #0891b2…) outside theme.ts; white glyphs on saturated squares read as an iOS home screen, not as CloseBoss.
  • Root Stack titles are English literals ("Tasks", "Lead", "Assistant").
  • Dark mode is well designed; older screens importing the legacy theme alias stay light.

09Visual design audit

Typography

Geist with cv11/ss03 alternates, negative tracking on headings, tabular numerals for stats: this is a considered scale and it reads well. Problems are in application, not definition: page titles vary between 20px (Settings), 24px (Contacts) and 30px (Manage AI Team); table headers mix 12px uppercase and 14px sentence case; the sidebar active label at 13.5px fails contrast on bg-slate-100 per axe.

Colour

Brand blue #0072ce is used for links, active states and primary buttons, but the visible primary action colour is black on Transactions, Calendar, Listings and Tasks, blue on Contacts/Ask Max, and amber-to-orange for Upgrade. Semantic colours are sound (emerald/amber/red) but two neutral families (Tailwind gray and slate) appear on the same screens: the Contacts stats cards are slate, the table below is gray. Orange text uses the AA-safe #b45309 correctly in Settings tabs.

Spacing and layout

Card padding varies between 12, 16, 20 and 24px; section gaps between 16 and 24. Content widths are set per page rather than by a layout token. The dashboard main pane uses a subtle gradient ground and cards use tinted shadows: keep.

Components

  • Buttons: ui/button.tsx is good (elevation lift, focus ring, five variants) and almost unused.
  • Inputs: no shared Input; borders alternate between border-gray-200 and border-slate-200, radii between lg and xl.
  • Badges/pills: Smart-list "DEFAULT" tag, rating pills, status pills and language pills each have their own padding and case.
  • Toggles: one Toggle component exists and follows the house rule. Adopt it everywhere.

10Information architecture audit

Web

  • Primary band (Ask Max, Conversations, Tasks, Leads, Calendar, Deals) is right. Rename "Leads" → "People" or rename the page to "Leads"; the nav and page must agree.
  • AI team band: each assistant nests Overview + Actions, so reaching "CMA" is Sales Assistant → Actions → CMA (three clicks). Flatten: the assistant row opens the hub; the hub shows actions as the first thing.
  • Settings is in three places: /dashboard/settings (4 tabs, ~20 panels), /account/profile (identity, branding), /dashboard/billing (under More). Group as Account · Team & AI · Channels · Messaging · Billing and put one "Settings" entry in the sidebar.
  • "More" holds Sales Model, Playbooks, Playbook runs, Skills, Coaching, Property Tools, Billing, Profile, Help. Billing and Profile are account items; Playbooks/Skills are power features. Split accordingly.
  • Command palette lists "Leads", "Contacts", "Inbox", "Performance", "Reports": four labels that no longer match the sidebar.

Mobile

Recommended tab bar: Boss · Inbox · Leads · Calendar · More. Team moves into Boss (the ribbon already shows it) and More. Tasks becomes a section of Calendar (the web calendar already merges them).

"If I am a new user, can I understand where I am?" On desktop, mostly yes, thanks to the sidebar bands. On mobile web, the drawer is the only map and it closes on every tap. In the native app, a cold start currently answers "no": no tab is highlighted.

15Dashboard audit

Ask Max (home)

Five-second test: fails today. The first paint is four tiles of "—", a one-line greeting, a collapsed "Business performance" row, then the composer. The proposals (the most important content) sit further down the thread. Recommend: proposals first, tiles as a compact strip with real zeros, composer docked at the bottom, thread history behind "Earlier".

Contacts

Opens with three chart cards (rating, last-contacted, growth) before the list. Agents open this page to find a person. Put search and the list first; charts behind an "Insights" toggle or in the Sales Assistant overview.

Tasks

Two chart cards, then tabs, then source filters, then search, then the table: four control rows before content. Collapse charts into a one-line summary ("38 done · 11 open · 3 overdue").

Manage AI Team

Good hierarchy: performance strip, per-assistant activity, roster. Bars have no axis or values; add hover values and a 14-day label. Grace and Oliver show "0 activities" with empty charts; show "Not set up yet" with a CTA instead.

Notifications

Three columns (hot leads, missed calls, reminders) with two empty. 434 unread with no read state, grouping or mark-all. Redesign as a single feed grouped by day with type chips and "Mark all read".

Mobile Boss

Agenda and alerts sit above the briefing, so proposals start below the fold. The command bar is already docked; show proposals first.

16AI UX audit

The model is right: Max proposes, the agent approves, assistants report back. The presentation undermines it.

  • Raw markdown. **Needs you:** is displayed literally in run reports. AiChatPanel has a MarkdownLite renderer; RunCard and the Boss thread do not use it. P0
  • Internal counters leak. "0/25 tools" with a progress bar tells the agent about a budget they never set. Show "Working · step 2 of 4" only while running; hide after.
  • Wrong framing for non-work. A clarifying question produced "Mission accomplished ★★★★★ · 0 steps complete · 6s". Only celebrate when outbound work completed; a question should render as a question card with a reply field.
  • Memory promise broken. Max says "each session starts fresh for me". A chief of staff remembers. At minimum show the last three exchanges as context and never let the model say it cannot remember.
  • Two AI surfaces. The floating bubble opens "AI Guide + per-contact SMS drafting" wearing Max's face; the page is "Ask Max". Retire the bubble on dashboard routes; keep a "Draft a text" action inside the contact drawer.
  • Transparency is good where it exists. Proposals show a reason and expected outcome. Extend the pattern: every run card shows Why → Did → Result → Your options.
  • Autopilot matrix is expressive but reads as an engineering table (assignee × channel × ask/auto). Present as sentences: "Emma may text back missed calls without asking."
  • Uncertainty. No confidence indicator on CMA values or lead scores beyond a number badge; add a range and a one-line basis.
  • Errors. Failed runs show status "failed" only; add what failed and a retry.
  • Mobile. Same thread with fewer states; "Bossy" and the app icon break persona continuity.

17Interaction design audit

  • Hover/press: Button lift and card lift are tasteful. Raw buttons lack them.
  • Focus: defined in the component library, absent in most pages.
  • Disabled: opacity-50 everywhere, no cursor or hint.
  • Loading: web has no route skeletons; each client fetches after mount, so pages paint empty then fill. Mobile skeletons are good.
  • Animations: sidebar chevrons, pulse dots, toast slide-in. Team ribbon pulse is permanent for "working" states and becomes noise.
  • Toasts: bottom-right, 4s, three tones, no dismiss button, no action ("Undo").
  • Confirmations: native confirm() in 38 places; wording inconsistent.
  • Empty states: a shared EmptyState exists (emoji icon, gray palette) but most pages write their own.
  • Feels static: Notifications, Listings. Feels uncertain: Ask Max run cards. Feels overcomplicated: Contacts, Settings.

24Recommended redesigns

A. Onboarding: one conversation, one outcome

Current problem

Welcome chat, Setup Wizard and Tour run in sequence, ask overlapping questions, and end on an empty dashboard.

Recommended architecture

Signup collects name, email, password. Max's welcome asks three things (market via the service-area picker, who you work with, first win) and then does one of two things: imports contacts (CSV or Google) or adds one lead by hand. The first proposal renders on the dashboard immediately from that data. Branding, voice, notifications and channels become "Set up later" cards Max proposes over the first week.

Layout

Full-screen conversation with a progress rail of three dots; each answer collapses into a chip; the final screen is the real dashboard with the first proposal highlighted.

User flow

Signup → 3 answers → import or add → dashboard with proposal → approve → first outbound message. Target: under 4 minutes, 12 interactions.

Mobile

Two value slides → login → same three answers inside Boss → notification permission requested when the first hot lead appears.

B. Ask Max thread and run cards

Current problem

Run cards expose engineering state (tool budgets, step counts, star ratings) and render text unformatted.

Recommended architecture

Four card types: Question (Max needs something; inline reply), Working (assignee avatar, current step, cancel), Approval (draft with edit/approve/reject), Done (what happened, artifacts, next options). Markdown rendered; one accent colour; no stars.

Layout

Proposals pinned at top; thread below; composer docked at the bottom with chips; context strip as a compact line ("2 hot · 1 appt · 1 deal · 0 deadlines").

Mobile

Identical card types; composer above tab bar; ribbon as an avatar row.

C. Leads list

Current problem

Charts before people; nine columns; eight icon actions; no phone layout.

Recommended architecture

Search + smart-list tabs + list. Columns: Name (with rating dot), Last activity, Stage, Next step, one primary action, overflow. Insights panel toggled from the header. Drawer stays.

Mobile

Cards; filter bottom sheet; swipe for text/call; "Add" floating above the tab bar.

D. Conversations

Current problem

Fixed three-pane; empty pane on phones; AI drafting lives in a separate bubble.

Recommended architecture

Two-pane on desktop; routed thread on mobile; reply box with "Draft with Max" and channel switch; call rows expand to transcript.

E. Settings

Current problem

Four opaque tabs and twenty panels; profile and billing elsewhere.

Recommended architecture

Settings index: Account (profile, branding, billing), AI team (voice, style, review policy, autopilot), Channels (phone, email, calendar, social), Messaging (templates, timing, drips), Data (import, smart link). Each a page with a sticky save state and a search box on the index.

F. Mobile home and tab bar

Current problem

Inconsistent landing; Team over Calendar; legacy hidden screens.

Recommended architecture

Boss · Inbox · Leads · Calendar (with Tasks) · More. Team roster inside Boss and More. Delete home/work/engage/analyze/manage routes after redirect mapping.

28Final strategic recommendations

  1. Treat Ask Max as the product. Every other screen is a record view; Max is the experience. Its rendering, states and memory deserve the first two sprints.
  2. Design mobile web and the native app as one mobile strategy. Today mobile web is a squeezed desktop and the native app is a separate product with its own names and colours. Share tokens, vocabulary and the Boss thread model.
  3. Make plan state a first-class UI input. Upsell, footer promos, credits, and plan names should all read from billing truth.
  4. Adopt before you add. The component library exists; the roadmap should ban new bespoke buttons, modals and confirms before building new features.
  5. Measure the first ten minutes. Instrument time-to-first-proposal and time-to-first-approval; they are the metrics onboarding should move.

—If I were the Head of Product

  1. Freeze new features for one sprint and ship the P0s: run-card rendering, mobile entry route, onboarding consolidation.
  2. Declare one vocabulary (Max, Leads, Conversations, Deals) and enforce it in nav, pages, palette, mobile and marketing in a single PR.
  3. Retire the floating AI Guide bubble on dashboard routes; move contact drafting into the drawer and the reply box.
  4. Rebuild Leads and Conversations for phones first, then let the desktop layouts inherit the card and thread components.
  5. Fund the design system as a product: one owner, a tokens package shared by web and mobile, lint rules that fail CI on drift.
  6. Make Max remember. Persist a rolling context and never let the model say it starts fresh; that sentence undermines the whole pitch.
  7. Key every upsell on plan truth and give paying users a calm, promo-free workspace.
  8. Collapse Settings into five groups with an index and search, and move Profile and Billing home.
  9. Cut the landing page in half and replace simulated proof with real proof or none.
  10. Put accessibility in the definition of done: axe in CI on the ten core routes, zero critical or serious violations, keyboard closable overlays.
